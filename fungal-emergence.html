Fungal Emergence

Arduino Uno, servo motors, air dry clay, acrylic paint, styrofoam, wire, bioplastic, fabric, felt, vinyl stickers, plastic 2024

Inspired by Anna Tsing’s “The Mushroom at the End of the World,” this piece reflects on the novel assemblages formed in the aftermath of a world not too different from the environmental conditions of our reality. In areas too toxic for human life, what new life emerges as a result of industrial disturbances?
