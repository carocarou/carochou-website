When Life Get's You Down

Risograph San Francisco Zine Fest 2018

As someone who struggles with chronic depression and anxiety, the artist created this interactive zine as a workbook for reframing mindsets learned while working in the field of community mental health. This zine is meant to be filled out like a journal, with guided reflection questions at the end.
