Bioremediation Game

p5.js interactive simulation Henaff Lab

Created with p5.js, this interactive simulation bridges scientific research with the community by allowing those unfamiliar with bioremediation to engage with the research currently underway at the Henaff Lab. Heavy-metal binding proteins are present in bacteria within the Gowanus Canal, a heavily contaminated waterway in Brooklyn, NY. By leveraging these proteins, a functional hydrogel can be developed to sequester contaminants such as lead, copper, and mercury.

In this simulation, you become the heavy-metal binding hydrogel that absorbs the lead in the Gowanus Canal. The game is connected to your laptop’s webcam which tracks your hand movement. The lead will be attracted to the hydrogel and can be removed when it is brought out of the water. If the lead touches certain bacteria, they will slow down. If the heavy metal interacts with bacteria again, it will die. Overtime, the canal microbiome will evolve with only lead-resistance bacteria (red) surviving.

Clean up the Gowanus Canal here. Please allow at least 30 seconds for the game to load.

Note: This is a simplified simulation of evolution. There will not just be 1 bacteria that is resistant. There will be assemblages.
